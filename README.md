# Xal's Warchest

Every weapon look you're still missing, and exactly where to get it.

Same idea as Xal's Armoire: shows what you're still missing and where it
comes from, without burying you in checkboxes. Armoire covers armor; Warchest
covers weapons. Works on Retail and on WoW Forever.

## What it does

- A Zone tab with only the weapons you can get right where you're standing,
  beside All, Raids and Dungeons tabs.
- Click any weapon for who drops it, who sells it, which quest gives it and who
  starts it, which profession crafts it, and which raid or dungeon boss drops it.
- Mark favorites with a right-click; they float to the top.
- A small chest on loot, group-loot rolls, vendor items and quest rewards for
  weapons you don't have the look for yet, plus a line on weapon tooltips.
- "Go to nearest vendor": an arrow, a minimap pin and a line to the closest
  vendor for your faction.
- A floating zone alert with a count, six color skins, and a window for what you
  already own.

## Optional add-on

Shortest Path Forever (WoW Forever only). With it installed, "Go to nearest
vendor" plans a real walking route to the vendor and draws it. Without it you
still get the arrow, minimap pin and line.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/xwc` | Show or hide the weapon tracker |
| `/xwc collection` | Open what you already own |
| `/xwc find <name>` | Look up a weapon by name |
| `/xwc options` | Open the settings window |
| `/xwc help` | List the commands |

## Installing

Install from CurseForge, or drop the `XalsWarchest` folder into
`World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\` (Retail) or the matching
`_forever_` AddOns folder (WoW Forever).
